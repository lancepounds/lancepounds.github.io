About Lance

Lance Pounds is an Omaha-based attorney, social entrepreneur, and policy writer who has contributed commentary and public policy research within Nebraska. He is particularly recognized for developing the Nebraska Technology & Freedom Policy Primer and writing about the regulation of artificial intelligence and emerging digital fields.

His legal experience includes special needs planning, Medicaid, and Social Security matters. That work informs his interest in how public policy affects people’s independence and everyday lives. He is interested in tools that help people communicate, participate in their communities, and exercise greater control over their lives.

Lance’s interest in technology builds on his legal work: both involve helping people navigate complex systems and make informed decisions. He is drawn to technology that makes information easier to understand and services easier to use. His background as an attorney also informs his approach to emerging technologies, connecting questions about innovation and regulation with their practical effects on people’s lives.

Across his legal, policy, and technology work, Lance combines strategic thinking, persistent client advocacy, and active community engagement. He strives to exceed expectations and build lasting relationships grounded in trust and shared progress. He believes collaboration, innovation, and empathy are essential to building stronger communities and supporting sustainable growth.

Law, technology & everyday independence

Legal rights matter most when people can use them. These projects connect my experience in disability law and policy with practical tools for communication, accessible interaction, and participation.

Lance OS

A personal web desktop bringing together my legal background, technology work, and creative projects. Its light and dark themes, larger text and controls, reduced animation, and single-click opening reflect my interest in giving people more ways to use technology.

Speechy

A communication board that speaks selected sentences aloud. It connects my interest in accessible technology with the practical need to be heard.

Zombie Trails

An old-school browser survival game following a journey from Omaha to Boise. Its development brings together branching decisions, resource management, interactive storytelling, and accessibility features, including dwell-to-tap controls.
