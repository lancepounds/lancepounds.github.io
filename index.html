Hi, I'm Lance Pounds.

I split my time between Omaha and Boise, and I learn to code by building things. My biggest project so far is Zombie Trails, a 1980s-style survival game about the drive between my two hometowns, if the drive happened during a zombie apocalypse.

It's in beta right now. Give it a run, and if something breaks or bores you, I want to hear about it.

I'm also building Speechy, a communication board that speaks a sentence out loud when you tap it. It's open on this desktop too.
